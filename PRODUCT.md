# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hospital social workers and discharge planners are the primary users — they work under time pressure on a hospital floor, managing multiple patients who need placement into post-acute care settings. Facility coordinators and platform administrators are secondary users who complete the workflow on the receiving end.

## Product Purpose

CareBridge eliminates delays in patient placement by giving social workers a clear, real-time view of their caseload, available facilities, and placement pipeline. Success means fewer patients waiting in beds past discharge, faster handoffs, and fewer information gaps between hospitals and care facilities.

## Brand Personality

Calm, competent, compassionate. The interface should feel like a trusted tool — not flashy, not cold, but quietly capable. Users are managing other people's care; the dashboard should never add cognitive friction.

## Anti-references

- Overly dense enterprise dashboards (SAP, Salesforce) — too much information, not enough clarity
- "SaaS cream" generic dashboards with gradient text, glassmorphism, and hero-metric templates
- Cold clinical interfaces that feel more like insurance or billing than patient care

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity before density** — Every screen should answer "what needs my attention?" in under 3 seconds. Whitespace is a feature, not waste.
2. **Progressive disclosure** — Surface the vital stats first; let users drill in when they need more. One layer deep at most for common tasks.
3. **Human-centered** — The data represents real patients and real care decisions. The tone, spacing, and visual hierarchy should reflect that gravity without being heavy.
4. **Role-fit, not one-size** — Different roles need different views. Each dashboard variant should feel purpose-built, not like a filtered version of the same page.
5. **Fast by feel** — Skeleton loading, no layout shift, instant responses. The perception of speed matters as much as actual performance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA minimum
- Full keyboard navigation for all interactive elements
- Dark mode support (already implemented)
- Reduced motion support for all animations
- Color is never the sole carrier of meaning (labels + icons alongside status colors)
